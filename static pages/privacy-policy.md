Last updated: 29 September 2026

SolarVy respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how SolarVy collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information when you use our website, energy assessment tools, reports, project services, installer introductions, Expert Review, financing-related services and other SolarVy services.

1. Who We Are

SolarVy is an energy intelligence platform helping individuals and businesses understand their energy needs, evaluate potential solutions and make more informed energy decisions.

For the purposes of applicable data-protection law, the relevant SolarVy legal entity will act as the data controller where it determines how and why your personal information is processed.

Legal entity: Solarvy Limited
Email: info@solarvy.ng

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use SolarVy, we may collect:

Contact information
Your name, email address, telephone number and other contact details.

Account information
Login details, account preferences and information associated with your SolarVy account.

Property and energy information
Property type, location, electricity usage, electricity expenditure, generator usage, fuel expenditure, operating hours, appliances or equipment, energy requirements and other information submitted during an assessment.

Project information
Your SolarVy assessments, recommendations, reports, installer requests, quotations, Expert Reviews, financing enquiries and project status.

Commercial information
For business customers, we may collect company name, facility type, business contact details and information relating to your organisation’s energy requirements.

Financing information
Where you choose to explore financing, we may collect information necessary to assess or facilitate your enquiry.

Where additional financial, identity or other information is required by a financing provider, you should be clearly informed what is being collected, why it is required and who will receive it.

Communications
Messages, enquiries, feedback and other communications you send to SolarVy.

Technical and usage information
We may collect information such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, interactions with SolarVy and similar technical information.

Payment information
Where you purchase a SolarVy service, payment information may be processed by our payment service providers. SolarVy should only retain payment information that is necessary for legitimate business, accounting and compliance purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use personal information to:

* provide and operate SolarVy;
* generate energy assessments and recommendations;
* create and manage your project;
* prepare reports and energy analysis;
* provide customer support;
* facilitate installer introductions;
* provide Expert Review services;
* facilitate financing enquiries you request;
* communicate about your assessment or project;
* process payments;
* improve SolarVy’s models, services and user experience;
* detect misuse, fraud or security threats;
* maintain appropriate business and regulatory records;
* comply with legal obligations; and
* send marketing communications where permitted.

We should not use personal information for purposes incompatible with those communicated to you without an appropriate legal basis.

4. Automated Analysis and Recommendations

SolarVy uses information provided by users together with energy data, engineering assumptions and analytical models to generate assessments, estimates and recommendations.

Some outputs may therefore be generated or supported by automated processing.
